## Source quality criteria

Preference order:

1. Peer-reviewed papers (venue + year noted)
2. Technical reports / model cards / system cards from primary orgs
3. Engineering documentation and postmortems
4. Regulatory filings, earnings transcripts (for incentive claims)
5. Reputable industry analysis — used only where 1–4 unavailable; flagged as secondary
